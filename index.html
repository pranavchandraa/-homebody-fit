Training rules

Rest: 60–90 sec after pull-ups and demanding sets; 30–60 sec for easier exercises.

Effort: Avoid training to failure every set. Keep 1–2 reps in reserve.

Progress: Add 1 rep at a time, or slow the lowering phase, when the current target feels manageable.

Recovery: Keep at least one day between strength sessions. Sleep 7–9 hours.

Fat loss: Ab exercises strengthen your core but do not selectively burn belly fat. Overall fat loss requires a sustainable calorie deficit.

Food basics

Include protein in each meal: eggs, milk, curd, paneer, dal, beans, soy or chicken.

Eat vegetables, fruit and enough carbohydrates to support training. Avoid crash diets.

A common muscle-building protein range for healthy adults who strength train is around 1.6–2.0 g per kg of body weight per day. Personal needs vary.

Cool-down · 3 min

Gentle hamstring stretch · chest stretch · quad stretch · shoulder stretch. Do not force any stretch.
